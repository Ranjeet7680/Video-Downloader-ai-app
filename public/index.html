📊 Resolution Guide

Video Quality Comparison: Low to High

Understand which resolution is best for your network speed and screen size

DATA SAVER (144p - 360p)

📱 Low / Mobile Friendly

Super fast downloads with minimal data usage. Perfect for slow 2G/3G connections or saving mobile gigabytes.

  • ✅ File size: ~2 MB - 8 MB
  • ✅ Instant loading & saving
  • ✅ Ideal for podcasts & voice-driven videos
RECOMMENDED (720p - 1080p)

🎬 High Definition (HD & FHD)

Crystal-clear visuals with vibrant colors and full stereo audio. The gold standard for phones, laptops, and sharing.

  • ✅ File size: ~15 MB - 40 MB
  • ✅ Sharp text, faces, and fast action
  • ✅ Merged high-bitrate stereo sound
CINEMA (1440p - 4K UHD)

🌟 Ultra High Definition

Flawless 2K & 4K fidelity with maximum bitrate. Best for large 4K TVs, monitors, and video editors.

  • ✅ File size: ~80 MB - 300+ MB
  • ✅ Maximum detail and high frame rates
  • ✅ Ideal for editing and archiving
AUDIO ONLY (MP3)

🎵 High Quality Audio

Extract crystal-clear 320kbps MP3 audio from any video. Keep music, talks, interviews, and sound effects.

  • ✅ File size: ~3 MB - 10 MB
  • ✅ Universal MP3 playback on all devices
  • ✅ Saves up to 95% storage compared to video

❓ Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does 1080p and 4K have sound included?

Yes! We use an integrated FFmpeg engine to automatically merge separate high-definition video streams with high-bitrate audio streams, so your downloaded 1080p or 4K video always has full stereo audio.

How do I download video in Low Quality to save mobile data?

Simply select 144p, 240p, or 360p (Data Saver) from the quality cards. These files are ultra-compact and download in seconds.

Can I download only the MP3 audio file?

Yes! Choose Audio Only (MP3) in the quality options. The tool extracts high-fidelity 320kbps MP3 audio directly from the video.

Where are the downloaded files saved?

Clicking "Save to Device" downloads the file directly into your browser's default Downloads folder on your phone or computer with a clean, readable filename.
